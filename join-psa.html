How to join the fair treatment PSA

This is a placeholder details page for the campaign. It outlines how hospitality businesses and customers could take part, support fair treatment, and help recognised establishments stand out in the community.

Business pledge

Businesses would complete a short pledge covering fair pay awareness, safe workplace practices, respectful rostering and visible support for hospitality staff.

Recognition badge

Participating establishments could receive a digital badge or window sticker showing they support decent work and shared growth.

Customer nominations

Customers could nominate venues that show respect for staff, respond well to concerns, and create a positive experience without relying on worker exploitation.

Campaign materials

Venues and supporters could access posters, social media tiles and simple prompts that explain why fair treatment in hospitality matters.
